Focal & backup persons

  • Focal person: signs in (Sign in → CD Focal Person) and updates their territory's status and CD Strategy details.
  • Backup person: updates the status only, with no sign-in needed.

To remove someone, clear their box and save. Every change is recorded in the territory's update history.

Update a CD Strategy

Who can update

  • Focal person: status and CD Strategy details
  • Backup person: status only
  • CWG leads and programme coordinators: their territories

To edit CD Strategy details, sign in first (Sign in → CD Focal Person or Admin).

Your email is recorded with every update.

Tip: in Google Maps, right-click the headquarters and click the numbers shown at the top to copy them.

Paste them into Latitude, and both boxes fill in automatically.
