Who can update CD Strategy details

Anyone can record a status update (their email is logged). Changing a territory's CD Strategy details needs one of these emails: the territory's focal person email (set in its details), its CWG lead, or a programme coordinator. Only signed-in coordinators see this panel. You can't remove your own email from the coordinators.
